# Product demonstrations and authored scenes

Use this built-in module for product illustrations, browser/app/phone compositions and animated walkthroughs. No external skill or kit is required. Reuse the actual project components or suitable bundled web-foundation fragments, then art-direct them for their placement. The scene is a believable explanation of one capability, not a substitute for a complete concept or functional application.

## Decide what deserves to be shown

First use [Visual medium and scene direction](visual-medium.md). Do not default to a handmade mini-application for every benefit. Choose an image, real product capture, authored illustration or hybrid from the communication job and reference. A mere icon plus caption, empty code boxes or category list is not a product demonstration. In image-led work, carry composed scenes into important body sections; the hero is not the whole art direction.

## Plan the still and the story

Record `capability and evidence | placement/visible size | focal object | supporting context | action | result | crop | compact adaptation | simulation boundary | poster | playback/reset`. A real product keeps its actual semantics, plausible data and recognizable content. An unbuilt capability is a proposal. An animated cursor is authored illustration, not proof that an action works.

Compose the settled result first. Give one object clear dominance; support objects explain a relationship instead of competing for attention. Build enough real interface context to identify the task. Keep meaningful words and values at the action and outcome. Simplify peripheral detail only inside the illustration; gray bars cannot replace pricing, primary proof or the content of a working app screen.

## Frame at the delivered size

- Design in the actual page slot with neighboring copy and background visible. A lovely isolated 1400-pixel composition can be illegible in a 400-pixel card.
- Use a purposeful crop or overlap to focus attention. Keep controls, labels, focal figures and outcomes inside the usable crop. Only nonessential continuation may bleed.
- Avoid a small whole window floating in a large empty plate. Enlarge the meaningful work region or choose a closer composition. Preserve believable chrome and proportions.
- Align secondary objects to meaningful edges. Do not obscure the main result with a decorative satellite, cursor, device bezel or badge.
- Recompose compact layouts: show the relevant panel or a sequential story rather than uniformly scaling desktop text down. A phone view is not a desktop screenshot in a phone frame.
- Fit real text before fixing box dimensions. Inspect row pitch, label/value grouping, insets, icon baselines, clipped shadows and floating status marks. A large headline number must keep its sign, unit and explanation aligned.
- A stylized scene can be expressive while keeping plausible content and coherent geometry. Do not remove all character in pursuit of minimalism.

## Choreograph cause and outcome

Use a small storyboard: establish context, act, show the system response, hold the useful result, then stop or reset with a visible cause. Determine reading time from actual content. A fixed ten-second loop is not mandatory. Every cursor movement must explain an action; measure targets from actual elements instead of hard-coding unrelated coordinates. Match cursor/touch portrayal to the illustrated platform.

Keep persistent objects recognizable across changes. A new list row makes room; a resized surface must not stretch its text. A changed value updates in a stable slot. Coordinate related movements so the outcome follows its cause. Keep user-operated playback separate from fictional scene controls. Essential real page actions must remain usable throughout.

Provide a useful static poster that communicates the outcome by itself. Reduced motion uses that composition or an appropriate quiet state sequence. Stop offscreen work where supported; give relevant autoplay pause/replay controls. A loop must return coherently, not erase completed work mysteriously while the viewer watches.

## Rehearse and repair

1. Inspect still frames at actual wide and compact placement sizes in the complete candidate.
2. Observe the entire playback, including every press, open surface, resize, value change and reset seam.
3. Inspect busy intermediate frames for clipping, collisions, unreadable overlap and incorrect targets.
4. Exercise pause/replay, repeated playback, resizing and reduced motion.
5. Correct the largest problem in this order: composition, hierarchy/readability, causal state changes, timing, finishing details. Capture the corrected version.
6. Record `version | runtime/URL | placement/size | state/time | capture | finding | correction | recheck`. If temporal inspection is unavailable, mark motion unverified.

Keep scene evidence separate from tests of real navigation, forms and app behavior. A React phone illustration does not verify Android/iOS behavior. Include the finished scene in every applicable complete hosted concept, keep preview links available and preserve the concept-selection boundary before integration.
