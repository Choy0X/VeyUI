# Landing-page content precedents

Reviewed 2026-11-08. These are qualitative observations, not evidence that a layout causes higher conversion. Public pages may change or vary by region. Text/content structure was inspected for all sources below; current desktop opening/supporting screenshots were additionally inspected for Linear and Notion. No full accessibility, motion or conversion audit was performed. Reuse these compact lessons without repeatedly fetching every site.

| Existing public example | Observed communication approach | Transferable decision | Avoid copying blindly |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | Names its product category and connects intake, planning, agent work and building to recognizable development activity. The inspected intake scene puts a concrete conversation alongside issue work | Organize capabilities around an actual lifecycle and demonstrate the handoff that makes them valuable | Its dark identity, agent positioning and developer context are not a universal SaaS template |
| [Notion Projects](https://www.notion.com/product/projects) | Relates configurable databases, timelines and charts to project work. Inspected supporting cards pair specific explanations with populated roadmap/tracker views | Translate each capability into a job and show the corresponding result; use detail when it helps evaluate fit | Do not copy its large inventory or customer proof into a smaller product without justification |
| [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/payments) | Groups payments around customer and business outcomes, then shows concrete checkout methods and offers a demo, with details for implementation choices | Connect a high-level benefit to mechanisms, examples and an appropriate next step | Their quantified claims and business reach belong to their evidence, not to another product |
| [Framer](https://www.framer.com/) | Connects creation, editable refinement and publishing, while showing the product workspace and providing a direct starting action | Tell a complete creation-to-outcome story and explain what remains in the user's control | A website-builder story or its AI promise does not belong on unrelated products |
| [Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/) and [project examples](https://basecamp.com/projects) | Uses recognizable coordination problems and specific project situations to explain how the product helps | Develop concrete use cases from real capabilities; write in the audience's working language | Its conversational length and tone are a brand choice, not a required style |

Usability foundation: [Nielsen Norman Group, Homepage Design: 5 Fundamental Principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/) emphasizes identifying the offering, showing relevant examples, supporting navigation/actions and communicating value. The practical inference for veyUI is to establish what the buyer needs to understand, then make text and visuals support that understanding. Famous branding alone does not establish persuasion.

## Apply the research to a project

1. Inspect the product, including capabilities missing from the existing public page.
2. Distinguish implemented code from released, accessible customer behavior.
3. Select useful, supported benefits for a specific audience.
4. Write several concrete narrative angles and choose an evidence-led argument.
5. Match each important claim to an actual demonstration, credible proof or a visible limitation.
6. Compose and inspect the full page, then test comprehension/conversion separately when possible.

These steps are an original synthesis. Use `product-story.md` for execution. Do not paste these brands' language into a new page or claim their commercial results for your design.
