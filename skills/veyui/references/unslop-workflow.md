# Remove genericness at its cause

Read this for new work, reworks and designs the user calls AI-looking. Use the quality contract and actual reference pixels. This workflow evaluates design decisions, not whether a forbidden visual motif appears.

## 1. Diagnose before restyling

Inspect the real artifact and state the failure mechanism. Use the appropriate category:

| Mechanism | Visible symptom | Repair at the right level |
|---|---|---|
| Interchangeable content | A competitor's logo could replace the product name without changing the page | Establish a concrete user problem, product action and honest proof; rewrite the message and structure around them |
| Template composition | Hero, arbitrary stats, equal cards, steps, testimonials and CTA repeat regardless of the offer | Assign each section a necessary communication job; choose its layout from that job |
| Strong hero, unfinished body | Cinematic opening followed by generic install/pair cards, blank inputs or lists masquerading as illustrations | Choose a suitable medium and art-direct each important supporting scene; calibrate the hardest one before expanding |
| Decorative identity | A glowing object, scenic background or accent color carries nearly all differentiation | Make content, geometry, interaction and product representation express the direction as well |
| Unfinished craft | Competing scales, poor wrapping, crowded labels, uneven gaps, clumsy icon/control geometry | Correct actual type, spacing and component relationships in the render |
| Implausible proof | Fake application chrome, inconsistent values, unexplained graphs, screenshot-shaped boxes | Build an accurate bounded product demonstration with coherent data and labels |
| Motion wallpaper | Every block enters the same way or moves without expressing a relationship | Assign purpose and hierarchy to motion, remove gratuitous loops and verify transitions over time |
| Library collage | Each imported component retains a different radius, font, shadow or interaction model | Keep proven behavior but normalize visual anatomy to one project system |

Choose the largest cause first, repair it at that level, then review the consequences. Old designs can retain strong parts; do not rebuild everything to prove effort.

## 2. Build a product-specific argument

For public pages, execute [Product story](product-story.md): inspect across the product, trace capabilities and availability, prioritize benefits and write complete messaging alternatives. Do not stop at a fact sheet or restyle old copy. For task UI, inspect the relevant routes, docs, assets, flows and data contracts and keep the language task-focused. Separate facts, proposals and missing evidence.

Draft content with composition. For each section or screen record:

**user question → specific answer → evidence/action → suitable visual structure → next step**.

A headline such as “Everything you need, in one place” needs a more specific answer. Reusing the same generic paragraph across all three concepts does not create three narratives. Facts stay invariant; framing, sequence and emphasis may vary deliberately. Do not demand invented uniqueness when the product shares common capabilities: clarity and task-specific detail can create a strong design.

Use a replacement thought experiment: if only the logo changed, what would still identify this product's job? Name actual visible evidence, not a slogan. This is a critique aid, not a requirement to make ordinary controls unfamiliar.

Art-direct a credible usage moment: one user/object, coherent time and status, a useful action and an understandable result. Make chart units and comparisons explicit. Check that sample alerts and urgency agree with the product promise and configured behavior. Do not use an ordinary neutral event as alarming proof without context. Group disclosures for a bounded demonstration when possible instead of scattering decorative sample-data badges on every fragment. Keep eligibility facts near the action they affect and purchase details near the purchase decision.

## 3. Resolve the visual direction before full-page repetition

Choose a lead reference and supporting references with explicit roles. Extract its relationships: dominant mass, text-to-proof ratio, light/dark distribution, alignment anchors, type character, detail density, material quality and motion hierarchy. Define concrete project tokens separately from estimates made from pixels.

Create a concept difference table across **composition, identity, UX, content framing and motion**. All three candidates must make meaningful choices across the relevant axes. Keep the same product truth, platform constraints and complete review scope. Do not force a novel navigation model where familiar navigation is essential.

Build a polished opening and defining task/section as an internal calibration step. Compare it to the selected reference before expanding. This early slice is not the user-facing concept deliverable. Complete the full landing page or representative end-to-end application task for every candidate before presentation.

## 4. Craft in three passes

Use [Seven craft passes](seven-craft-passes.md) across these three review scales. Carry the planned relationships into the render and record the corrections in the existing concept review.

### Macro: silhouette, hierarchy and rhythm

Review full-page/task captures, then the opening at readable scale. Identify the primary focal point; check the proportion of type, product evidence and whitespace. Assign sections different compositions suited to their messages, with deliberate density changes. Avoid making “large headline plus huge full dashboard” the solution to every brief. On mobile, recompose proof into a useful compact view instead of shrinking every desktop detail or pushing the narrative several screens down.

### Meso: component relationships

Before polishing components, verify that this section needs those components at all. Read `visual-medium.md` for image-led scenes. Replacing an intended image or composed product render with a miniature form is a medium/composition error, not a padding issue. Inspect the weakest important section independently and repair it before recommending the candidate.


Inspect shared edges, padding, baselines, text measure, icon/label pairs, border strength, radius relationships and action hierarchy. A pill can be correct; six competing badges can still obscure the message. A bento can work; unrelated cards with equal weight can still fail. Ensure the component's data and behavior explain why it has that shape.

Use custom composition over existing accessible primitives. Do not inherit a library's demo appearance by accident. Review default, hover/press, focus, selected, disabled and relevant error/loading states in their actual context.

### Micro: finishing details

Check font loading, wrapping, truncation, numeral alignment, optical centering, corner clipping, focus clearance, image crop, asset edges, hover transitions and motion endpoints. Check start, intermediate, interrupted and settled animation states. A polished static frame cannot excuse broken animated digits or layout jumps.

Do not add flourishes until macro and meso issues are resolved. Once corrected, re-render affected sizes/states rather than trusting source changes.

## 5. Judge fit and execution independently

Use a short observation table, not a made-up quality score:

**dimension | target/reference | actual evidence | meets / needs work / unverified | correction | recheck**.

Assess composition, product specificity, typography, spacing, palette/materials, component craft, asset integration, motion, responsive adaptation and task clarity. Identify blockers and major defects separately from minor preferences. A different identity is not automatically a better identity.

Seek independent review according to `review-team.md`. Fix in-scope material findings autonomously before showing the set. If a real access, asset or product-fact blocker prevents a proper result, disclose it as a blocker. If repeated changes stop improving the evidence, reassess the direction or dependency rather than perform endless cosmetic loops. Do not silently lower the bar or assert “professional” based on iteration count.

## 6. Protect the selected result through integration

Keep the selected render/version and its tokens, assets, component anatomy, flow and motion decisions together. Integrate in the real stack without replacing those decisions with convenient defaults. Compare the integrated output against the approved concept at matched sizes/states. Verify real routes, content, data, accessibility and performance separately. The approved prototype is a visual contract; production behavior still needs real implementation evidence.

## Optional aids, not taste certificates

Use the built-in Craft and Interface audit modules alongside the project’s actual code/browser checks. Resolve installed tools and version-specific commands before execution. Do not invent commands, run unavailable hooks, or evade browser/tool restrictions. A detector warning is a finding to investigate in context; a clean detector result does not prove that the design matches the reference. Never falsify tool receipts or disable a valid check just to obtain a passing report.
