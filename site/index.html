Stop shipping AI-looking interfaces.

veyUI gives your coding agent a real design process: study references, build three finished concepts, wait for your pick, then implement and verify.

Install veyUI
npx skills add Choy0X/VeyUI

Free and open source. MIT.Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot and more

Why it happens

Same prompt, same page.

Ask an agent for a landing page and you get a centered headline, three cards and a gradient. Not because it cannot design, but because nothing makes it decide anything.

Opinion, not a statistic. The tiles above are an illustration.

The run

Six steps between your prompt and your code.

  1. Reads your project to find what the product really does, then turns capabilities into honest copy.

  2. Proposes three directions, each with a real public example you can open and a tradeoff.

  3. Builds three distinct, complete concepts, hosts them locally and has them reviewed.

  4. You pick one. Nothing is integrated before that.

  5. Builds the chosen design in your real stack, web, mobile or desktop.

  6. Inspects the rendered result and reports what was and was not checked.

The result

Same product. Better decisions.

Left is what an agent ships when nothing makes it decide. Right is the same product after veyUI studied references, built three concepts and let you pick one.

Slackwater is a fictional product, designed with veyUI as an example. The tide data is sample data.

  1. 1

    Type

    WasSystem sans in one heavy weight, a gradient word in the headline, grey body copy.

    NowOne light grotesk with tabular figures, the headline in two tones, mono only for the smallest labels.

  2. 2

    Layout

    WasA flat centered stack: text, a button, then a row of cards.

    NowA scene: the headline over a horizon and a dashboard docked to the bottom. Tight spacing inside a group, wide spacing between groups.

  3. 3

    Color

    WasAn indigo to violet gradient, a glowing orb and pastel cards.

    NowA navy dusk ground with one amber signal, kept for the go window and the horizon glow.

  4. 4

    Proof

    WasThree identical icon cards with a buzzword each and nothing to check.

    NowThree readings (a 24 hour dial, a swell rhythm, a wind gauge) and the real tide curve with the go window marked, all sample data.

  5. 5

    Copy

    WasSupercharge your surf planning with AI.

    NowSurf the 2 hours that matter. It names the audience, the benefit and the action, and every number on the page is sample data that says so.

veyUI builds three of these. You choose one.

    Limits

    What it will not promise.

    It will not

    • It cannot guarantee great design.
    • Final output still needs real rendered inspection and your choice.
    • 31 of its concept studies are text only because image rights are unverified.
    • Image generation always needs your permission.

    It needs

    • Python 3.10+
    • Node 22+
    • Chrome, Edge or Chromium for web checks
